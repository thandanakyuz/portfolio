About Me

Hi! I'm T. Handan Akyuz (they/them), an Learning Experience Designer and e-Learning Specialist based in Edmonton, AB. I'm passionate about creating inclusive, accessible learning experiences that promote social justice and equity in education. With a Master's degree in Educational Research (Curriculum & Learning) from the University of Calgary and experience designing online courses for non-profits and private colleges, I bring a learner-centered, anti-oppressive perspective to every project.

My work spans curriculum design, course development on multiple LMS platforms (Moodle, D2L, Articulate Rise 360), multimedia content creation, and educational technology implementation. My research interests include intersectionality, anti-oppression education, critical social justice, gender & sexuality studies, adult learning, learning experience design, and eLearning.
