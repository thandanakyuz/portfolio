Anderson College — Curriculum-Aligned eLearning

Designed and developed multiple curriculum-aligned eLearning courses for a private career college in Toronto, built on D2L Brightspace and Articulate Rise 360 with an accessibility-first framework and rich multimedia.

Client
Anderson College of Health, Business & Technology
Role
eLearning Designer & Curriculum Developer
Timeline
April 2024 — October 2024
Location
Toronto, ON

Overview

Anderson College offers career-ready training in healthcare, business, and technology. My work focused on designing curriculum-aligned eLearning courses for adult learners enrolled in their career programs — ensuring alignment with adult learning principles, accessibility standards, and institutional learning outcomes.

The role spanned course design, LMS administration, multimedia content production, and applied research into emerging instructional design and educational technology practices.

Responsibilities

  • Developed accessible learning materials and activities for program delivery.
  • Provided D2L LMS organization, maintenance, and technical solutions.
  • Collaborated with faculty and instructional designers on creating and refining courses.
  • Researched, analyzed, and implemented innovations in educational and technological practice.
  • Produced multimedia content — animated explainers, AI-narrated walkthroughs, and infographics — to enhance engagement and learning outcomes.

Tools & Software

  • H5H5P

Approach

Interactive H5P modules were built within MoodleCloud's embedded H5P builder to deliver formative course activities. Animated explainers were produced in Vyond, visual design in Canva Pro, and audio/video walkthroughs in Descript and WellSaid Labs. The overarching emphasis was on accessible instructional design and the practical implementation of emerging educational technologies in an online learning environment.
